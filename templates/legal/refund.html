Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our commitment to fair, transparent transactions and ethical pet procurement.

We process all verified refunds within 5-7 business days.

1. Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order at any time before the pet is handed over to our verified delivery partners. When you successfully cancel a paid order, your payment status will immediately transition to Refunded.

Automatic Process: Once triggered, the entire transaction amount including GST and handling fees is queued for automatic processing back to your original payment method.

2. Eligible Reasons for Refund

  • Health Concerns: Upon arrival, if the pet exhibits pre-existing health issues documented by a certified veterinarian within 48 hours.
  • Seller Revocation: If PIBA forcibly deactivates a seller due to guideline violations, all active unfulfilled orders are automatically cancelled and 100% refunded to buyers.
  • Logistical Failure: If delivery fails due to circumstances explicitly within our or our partners' control.
  • Buyer Cancellation: Pre-shipment voluntary cancellations initiated from the "My Orders" dashboard.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Due to the ethical and logistical complexities of live animal transport, refunds are not provided in the following scenarios:

  • Buyer remorse after the pet has been shipped or delivered.
  • Failure of the buyer to be present at the delivery address after multiple attempts.
  • Incompatibility issues (we highly recommend utilizing our matching algorithm and speaking with sellers extensively before purchase).

4. How to Request a Post-Delivery Refund

If you meet the eligibility criteria for a post-delivery refund (e.g., medical reasons):

  1. Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Provide official documentation from a registered veterinary clinic.
  3. Our team will arrange for the safe retrieval of the pet and process your refund immediately upon verification.

5. Contact Information

For any questions regarding refunds, contact our dedicated support team at support@piba.com.
